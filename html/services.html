What we make

Four kinds of work leave this workshop. All of it is made to order — nothing here is stocked off a shelf.

Custom knives

Kitchen, hunting or everyday-carry blades built around how you actually use a knife — handle shape, balance and edge geometry all discussed before we forge anything.

From £180

Tool restoration

Axes, hand planes, garden tools and other steel that's earned a second life: rust removed, edges reground, handles refitted.

From £45

Ironwork & hardware

Gates, hooks, fireplace tools and door hardware, hand-scrolled and finished to match the rest of your build.

Quoted per piece

Blacksmithing classes

One-day taster sessions and weekend intensives for anyone who wants to leave with a bottle opener, a hook, or a lot of new respect for the craft.

From £95 / person
