Bladesmithing & blacksmithing, Thornbury

Steel that is shaped by hand, not by a catalogue.

Every knife, hook and gate that leaves this workshop is heated, hammered and finished by one pair of hands. We take on a small number of commissions a month so each piece gets the time it actually needs.

Forged in Thornbury

Every piece is made in our workshop on the edge of town — no outsourced casting, no imported blanks.

02

Steel with a history

We work mostly in recycled leaf-spring and reclaimed tool steel, so each blade carries a little of where it came from.

Sharpening for life

Bring any blade we've made back for a free sharpen, any time it starts to lose its edge.
